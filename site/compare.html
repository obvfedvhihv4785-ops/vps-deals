Side by side

"From" is the cheapest monthly figure stated on the provider's page. Currencies are not converted — no exchange rate is applied, because that would make the numbers less true, not more. Rows are grouped by currency and sorted by price within each group, so the # column is a rank inside that currency only: a EUR row is never ranked against a USD row, and no claim is made about which currency is worth more.

The Term column is the part most price tables leave out. Not every figure written "/mo" is a month-to-month price: some are the rate you get for prepaying two years, and one is a rate that only holds for the first three months. 10 of the 25 priced providers below are not quoting a plain monthly price. Two numbers in this column are only comparable when their terms match, so read the Term column before reading the price. Where a provider states no term at all, this column says not stated — it does not assume the friendliest answer.

Renews at is the figure the advertised rate becomes later. 8 of the 25 priced providers state one next to their headline, and it is often a large jump — HostGator (from $2.09 to $4.68/mo) is the largest here. A deals page that shows only the introductory number is showing half the offer, so both figures are published side by side and each is quoted from the provider's own page.

What this comparison does not do

  • It does not rank providers by quality — only by the price their own page states.
  • It does not normalise specs. A $2 plan and a $40 plan are not the same product, and the cheapest figure is usually a small entry plan.
  • It does not convert currencies. USD and EUR entries are listed as published.
  • It does not compare prices with different terms as though they were alike. Where a provider's rate depends on a commitment — a 24-month prepay, a first-three-months promotion, or a page that offers several terms without saying which one the price belongs to — the Term column says so, and says nothing when the page says nothing.
  • It does not treat the advertised rate as the price you will pay. Where the provider states a renewal figure, that figure is published beside it in the Renews at column, taken from the same sentence on the same page.
  • It does not fill gaps. A provider with no readable price shows a dash, never an estimate.
