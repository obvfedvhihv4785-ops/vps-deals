How to read this table

  • verified — the number was in the page's HTML on the date shown, and the quoted text is displayed on the entry.
  • stale — the last refresh failed for this provider. The previously verified price is kept and labelled with the date it was verified. It is never shown as current.
  • no readable price — the page loaded but exposed no monthly price in its HTML (usually JavaScript-rendered), or it blocked the request. No number is published.
  • Intro prices — where a provider advertises a discounted first term and a higher renewal rate, both appear on the entry. The headline figure is the cheapest price the page actually states.
